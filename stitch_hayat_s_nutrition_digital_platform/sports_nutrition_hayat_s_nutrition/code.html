Maintain output and delay fatigue. Crucial for sessions lasting longer than 60 minutes.

Post-Workout

Kickstart recovery and muscle repair. The "anabolic window" prioritizes protein synthesis.

  • egg_alt
    Protein 20-40g high-quality protein within 2 hours
  • local_drink
    Fluids Replace 150% of fluid weight lost
  • bakery_dining
    Carbs Restore depleted glycogen stores
bedtime

Rest-Day Nutrition

Recovery happens on rest days. While caloric needs may drop slightly, micronutrient demands remain high. Focus on anti-inflammatory foods, sustained protein intake for tissue repair, and maintaining adequate hydration even without the stimulus of exercise.
